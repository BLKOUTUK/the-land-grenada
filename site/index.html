The Land · Simon, St Andrew

One site, one operator, one plan

A two-minute walk through the whole site: the house, the warehouse kitchen and the mango grove suites.

What is on the site

  • The house, renovated, and let only as one whole house to two couples, for about twenty weeks a year. The rest of the year it is the family's home.
  • The warehouse, converted to a commercial kitchen: garden dining for up to 30 and a takeaway window to the road.
  • Three guest suites in the mango grove, built from flat-pack modules on screw piles round one shared yard.
  • Later: a farm school, arts and craft workshops, and a catering business.

The renders and plans are concepts from October 2026. Layouts are not final.
