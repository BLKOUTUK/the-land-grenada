# Existential Plane

Why are we doing this and what does it mean?

## The Core Proposition

The Land is not just a holiday rental. It is:
- A family property being reclaimed
- A learning curve in hospitality
- A potential eco-retreat
- A site of cultural production
- A legacy project

## Film Ambitions

- **Kit**: phone, drone, Zoom recorder, lights
- **Subject**: the process of becoming a maker, not just a facilitator
- **Funding**: if process goes well, BFI (Rob is Chair of Doc Society)
- **Links to**: The Exodus Collective (Zoe), community-led arts

## Positioning

What makes The Land distinctive rather than generic?
- Black queer owned in the Caribbean
- Returning-diaspora narrative
- Community-building, not extraction
- Low-impact, learning-out-loud ethos

## The Uncomfortable Questions

- Is this project for Rob, for the family, for the community, or for guests?
- What happens if 30% occupancy never comes?
- What does "eco-retreat" actually mean here?
- How does the film serve the project, and vice versa?
