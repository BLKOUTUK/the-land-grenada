The money, in two lines

  • The house, complete, costs £95,500 to £114,075. Against £98,000 that's anywhere from £2,500 to spare to £16,075 short. Between 41% and 48% of it comes from real quotes.
  • The business is mine. Phase 1 (the kitchen in the old warehouse and the first two guest suites in the mango grove) opens with the house; Phase 1b (the third suite, the pergola and bar, the pool) follows as it earns. It sits in the back garden and leaves the farmed land alone. The pool, group bookings and retreats make the house easier to let, so we get repaid sooner. Funding it is my job.

The four questions for tonight

  1. Is this the right shape? Seven shares of £14,000, with at least £7,000 back, and up to all £14,000 if we choose. If someone can only put in less, how should their share work? And should we ask Mum and Dad, and the next generation?

  4. What goes in the house company's rules? The shares, repayment, the fund, how we decide, and what happens if someone wants out.
