4 · The offer

Positioning, settled from the neighbourhood itself, not from a mood board: artists, women, dining, live music, diaspora, culture, under fairy lights. Three real venues already operate nearby — a Friday nightclub, a Friday–Saturday restaurant-with-a-party-side, a daily sports bar — none serving this register.

The daily menu carries a second, genuinely urgent mission, not just differentiation: seasonal, more fish and vegetarian, less meat, less fried — traditional Caribbean food done well, not an imported health-food trope. Grenada's own numbers are real and serious (59.9% of adults overweight or obese, NCDs account for 82.8% of deaths), and a real government drive already exists (NCNCDC, GFNC, National Nutrition Week each June) to eventually align with, once there's a track record to show them. Cookery classes are the natural extension, using the same Studio infrastructure already planned for artists' workshops. Full grounding in seamoon-neighbourhood-intelligence.md.

6.3 Accommodation — three L-suites, seasonal

Three suites, each sleeping up to 6: the T (two suites) trades from the start of year 1 and the L (third) from year 2, as the Sheet has it. Month 6 was the earlier assumption, but the L needs a Kickstarter (~3 months to raise) and then manufacture and shipping, so year 2 is the better-supported date. Price per suite-night = B×S + E×(guests−2), B = US$100 (couple, off-peak) rising 5% a year, E = US$25 per extra guest. That is £75 for a couple off-peak and £169 for a family of four at peak (US$225) in year 1. Peak is carnival and July/August, ~9 of 52 weeks, at the 1.75× premium and 51% occupancy; off-peak occupancy is solved to reconcile with the known 33.5% annual average (29.8%), not guessed. Families at peak (4 guests), couples the rest of the year. A separate maturity ramp (50%/75%/100% by year 3) sits on top, directionally supported by the hospitality research's own "occupancy stabilises slowly" finding. Year 5 rises only with the 5% rate increase because capacity is fixed at three suites.
