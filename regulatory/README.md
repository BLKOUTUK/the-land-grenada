# Regulatory Plane

What rules govern what we can do at The Land?

## Scope

- Planning permission for guest accommodation
- Building codes and renovations
- Tourism licensing (Grenada Tourism Authority)
- Tax obligations: income, property, VAT
- Labour law for local staff
- Environmental compliance

## Key Authorities

- Grenada Tourism Authority (GTA)
- Planning & Development Authority
- Inland Revenue Division
- Ministry of Environment
