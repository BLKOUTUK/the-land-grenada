# The Land — Research & Learning Vault

Seven-acre coastal property in Simon, St Andrew Parish, Grenada.

## 5 Analytical Planes

- **regulatory/** — What rules govern what we can do?
- **economic/** — What does it cost and what might it earn?
- **geographical/** — What is the land, climate, ecology?
- **relational/** — Who are the key people and how do we relate?
- **existential/** — Why are we doing this and what does it mean?

## 3 Support Planes

- **networking/** — Mapping and building relationships
- **data-capture/** — Research tools, filming schedule, kit
- **logistics/** — Travel, accommodation, health, insurance

## Principles

- Open-source or one-off tools; no vendor lock-in
- Markdown + CSV as canonical format
- Version-controlled and fully portable
- Text-first, low-bandwidth for Grenada

2 June – 14 July 2026 (on-island)
